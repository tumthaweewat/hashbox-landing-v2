Ranking guarantees: what the wording actually means

  • Promises page one / Top 10 on the agency's own page: Metier ("Top 10 within 90 days"), IBEX ("Page 1 Guarantee within 90 days"), MakeWebEasy (at least four Top-10 keywords in the 12-month package) and Cotactic ("page one in 6 months" listed as a package item)
  • Conditional KPI guarantee — a stated target, and you don't pay or they keep working for free if it's missed: Primal, Minimice, Hashbox
  • Explicitly no ranking guarantee: Hashbox, WarriorSEO, Relevant Audience

A page-one promise is the claim to read most carefully: ask *which* keywords (low-competition terms with no searches are easy), how "page one" is measured and on what day, and what happens if it isn't reached. A conditional KPI guarantee is easier to check because the target and the consequence are written down.

7 questions to ask before you sign

  1. What does the price include, and what is the minimum term? If it isn't on the website, get it in writing.
  2. If you guarantee page one, for which keywords, measured how, and what happens if you miss?
  3. What do you do in month one? A good answer starts with an audit and technical fixes, not "eight articles."
  4. Where do backlinks come from? Bought links from private networks can get a site penalised.
  5. Can I see the ranking data myself, daily — and who keeps it if I leave?
  6. How do you measure AI search? Ask to see a real report of AI Overview and ChatGPT citations, with dates and platforms.
  7. Can I talk to one current client? If the case studies are anonymous, ask for a name.

FAQ: Hiring an SEO agency in Bangkok

How much does SEO cost in Bangkok in 2026?

Among agencies that publish prices, starting retainers run from THB 29,900 to 39,000 per month (Hashbox, WarriorSEO, ANGA). Relevant Audience publishes a wider range of THB 20,000–150,000+ per month with typical campaigns at 40,000–60,000, and MakeWebEasy sells packages from a THB 22,000 one-off service to THB 320,000 for 12 months. Most agencies don't publish a price.

Can an SEO agency guarantee page one on Google?

No agency controls Google's ranking algorithm. Four of the 13 agencies we checked still promise page one or Top 10 on their websites. A conditional KPI guarantee — a written target with a stated consequence, such as working for free — is easier to verify than a ranking promise. Always ask which keywords a guarantee covers.

Who are the best SEO agencies in Bangkok?

On seven criteria anyone can check on each agency's website (published price, no ranking guarantee, technical-first, AI search, live measurement, named cases, data ownership), the highest scores in October 2026 were Hashbox (6.5/7, our own agency), Relevant Audience (6/7), and ANGA, Primal and Minimice Group (5/7). The right choice depends on your budget and how much team size and awards matter to you.

Do I need GEO or AI search optimization as well as SEO?

Most of the work overlaps — a fast, crawlable, well-structured site with clear facts helps both. What AI search adds is measuring whether ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews mention or cite you, and getting listed in the directories and sources those assistants draw on. 12 of the 13 agencies we checked now offer it, so ask how they measure it.

How long does SEO take to show results?

On a technically sound site, impressions often move within one to two months and rankings for moderately competitive terms within two to four months. Competitive head terms where large agencies compete can take six to twelve months. A good agency tells you which keywords are quick and which are slow after the audit.

Is ChatGPT's recommendation of an SEO agency reliable?

Treat it as a shortlist, not a verdict. In 45 answers to "Which SEO agency in Thailand is the best?" the most-cited sources were press-release pages, agency directories and lists published by the agencies themselves. An AI answer shows who is visible, not who delivers results.
